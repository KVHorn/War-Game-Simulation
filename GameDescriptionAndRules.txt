GAME TITLE: Seek and Destroy

Players:

Competing Players: 2+
Minimum Total Players Required: 3
Note: The game cannot be played without a game master.

Game Setup:

Board(s):

Galaxy Map:
    - A map of the entire galaxy split into a number of sections dependant on the 
    number of players.
    - This map should be large enough to show mission movements but shouldnt take 
    up a huge amount of space.

Planet/Moon Maps:
    - Planet & Moon maps are randomly selected at time of first encounter/exploration.
    - Planet Maps are the largest maps/boards in the game.
    - Moon Maps are at max 0.25x the size of planet maps.
    - Energy Farm map/diagram - smallest possible map type. 
        - (Might just be a small circle spinner device)
